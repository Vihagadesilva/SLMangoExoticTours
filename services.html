How Vehicle Enquiries Work

Booking private transport with SL Mango Exotic Tours is clear, honest, and straightforward.

1

Submit Your Proposed Route or Dates

Use our enquiry form or reach out via WhatsApp with your flight arrival time, group size, and planned stops (e.g. Airport → Kandy → Ella → Galle → Airport).

2

Receive an All-Inclusive Transparent Quote

We provide a comprehensive quote covering vehicle rental, fuel, driver meals, driver accommodation, highway expressway tolls, and vehicle parking. No surprise fuel surcharges or hidden mileage limits.

3

Meet Your Chauffeur at the Airport

Upon arrival at Bandaranaike International Airport, your assigned licensed chauffeur-guide greets you at the passenger arrivals exit with your name paging board and fresh king coconut water.
