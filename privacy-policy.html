Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective for all visitors and clients of SL Mango Exotic Tours.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, contact us via WhatsApp, or send an email, we collect the personal information you voluntarily provide. This typically includes your full name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, tentative travel dates, party size, and itinerary preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for the purpose of communicating with you, designing custom itineraries, calculating transport quotes, and coordinating chauffeur and travel logistics in Sri Lanka. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data to external third-party advertisers or commercial brokers.

3. Static Hosting & Security Architecture

This website operates as a client-side static site hosted on Hostinger. We do not store sensitive payment card details or passport numbers on unencrypted web servers. Any formal confirmations, passport scans required by wildlife park authorities for permits, or flight details are handled privately through encrypted email or direct messaging.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website includes links to WhatsApp (Meta), Google Fonts, and cdnjs (for GSAP animation libraries). When clicking external links, their respective privacy policies apply.

5. Contacting Us Regarding Your Data

If you wish to review, update, or request the deletion of any personal communications or contact records we hold, please email us directly at explore@slmangoexotictours.com.
