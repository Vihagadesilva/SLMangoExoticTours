Terms & Conditions

Clear, honest booking parameters governing our travel coordination and private chauffeur agreements.

1. Nature of Proposed Itineraries

All itineraries published on this website represent proposed sample routes. They become confirmed travel packages only upon mutual agreement on finalized dates, passenger numbers, vehicle class, and formal written confirmation by SL Mango Exotic Tours.

2. Quotations & Price Integrity

Our quotes for private vehicle rental and chauffeur services include the vehicle, professional driver allowance, fuel, highway expressway tolls, and vehicle parking. Entrance fees to national parks, cultural monuments, train tickets, and meals are either specified as included or excluded in writing prior to confirmation.

3. Railway & Weather Contingencies

The scenic highland mountain railway (Kandy – Nuwara Eliya – Ella) is operated by the Government Department of Sri Lanka Railways. Seat reservations open 30 days in advance. While we make every professional effort to secure preferred reserved observation carriages, in the rare event of government train cancellations or line maintenance, our private chauffeur and executive vehicle will navigate the panoramic mountain road seamlessly.

4. Travel Insurance Requirement

While our vehicles carry full passenger insurance required by Sri Lankan transport law, all international travellers are strongly advised to secure comprehensive personal travel and medical insurance covering flight delays, personal belongings, and medical emergencies.

5. Governing Law

All direct service agreements are governed by the applicable laws of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.
